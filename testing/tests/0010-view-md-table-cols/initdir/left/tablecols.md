# Stage moves

| Stage | Move | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Analyze | Read the client's own design first. The client's notes, prototypes and backlog often already say how the client wants the work done. | The answer to "how should fields be mapped" was in the design notes of the client. |
| Analyze | Look at a comparable tool that the client already uses or can buy. | The tool gave the FDE the idea that set the delivery surface: an Excel add-in. |
| Ideate | Bring your own ideas and develop them in a long conversation with the model, then test sketches with the decider. | The decisive idea came from the FDE. Objections such as "that's ETL" changed the idea in one evening. |
